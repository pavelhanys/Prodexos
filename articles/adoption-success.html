Why adoption is part of every successful project

A project is not complete when a solution is delivered. It succeeds when people can use the change with confidence.

Adoption starts early

Successful teams identify impacted groups, process changes, capability gaps and leadership messages while delivery is still being shaped.

Delivery and change belong together

Adoption work should not sit at the edge of a project. It informs requirements, sequencing, communication, training and transition planning.

Practical takeaway

Every delivery plan should include an adoption plan that makes ownership, readiness, training and behaviour change visible from the start.
